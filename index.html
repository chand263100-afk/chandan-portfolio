About Me

Editing isn't just cutting clips — it's telling a story.

I'm Chand Verma, a professional video editor currently based in Kota, crafting cinematic long-form content for creators and brands. Before that, I spent 1.5+ years building my craft in Chandigarh — learning to read footage for rhythm, not just content.

How A Project Moves

My Editing Process

From raw footage to delivery — the same six steps, every time.

i.

Research

Understanding the story, the audience, and the feeling we're going for.

ii.

Storyboarding

Mapping structure and pacing before a single cut is made.

iii.

Editing

Building rhythm — cutting for emotion, not just chronology.

iv.

Sound Design

Layering music and effects so the film feels as good as it looks.

v.

Color Grading

Giving the footage its final cinematic mood and tone.

vi.

Delivery

Export, review, and final polish — ready for the world to see.
